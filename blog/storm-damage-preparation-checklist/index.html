Storm Damage Preparation Checklist in Draper, UT

Home / Blog / Preparing Your Home for Storms in Draper, UT

In Draper, UT, a storm can leave homes and businesses with devastating water damage. Getting ready ahead of the season, and again ahead of each storm, can significantly cut both the damage to your property and what restoration costs later. This checklist from Draper Restoration covers everything Draper homeowners should do to prepare.

Pre-Season Preparation

Complete these tasks before storm season begins: Inspect your roof for damaged, missing, or worn shingles and flashing. Clear out every gutter and downspout, then confirm water discharges the way it should. Go around all exterior penetrations, doors, and windows, checking and sealing as you go. Make sure the sump pump passes inspection and its backup power system works in a test. Review your insurance coverage including flood and wind damage. Document your property with current photos for insurance records. Trim trees near your home to reduce wind-driven branch damage.

Before a Storm Event

With a storm forecast, first secure anything loose outside, since it could become a projectile. Shut the storm shutters, or put boards over windows, when the forecast warrants it. Ensure your sump pump is operational and the battery backup is charged. Move valuables and important documents to upper levels or waterproof containers. After the Storm Ends

Once it has passed, begin by inspecting the property from somewhere safe, and do not go in until you have. Take pictures and video of everything that was damaged. Cover roof openings with tarps if safe to do so. Do not enter flooded areas until electrical hazards are assessed. Contact your insurance company to report damage.
