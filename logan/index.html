Where Water Damage Starts in Logan Properties

Walk through a house from the roof down and you pass most of the places a leak can begin. That is the route we take when inspecting a property in Logan, and five spots along it account for most calls.

Attics: The top of the house takes on water when shingles are damaged or the seal around a vent pipe gives out. Insulation acts like a sponge, so it can hold a surprising amount before any of it shows on the ceiling. Wet insulation stops insulating and keeps the wood beneath it damp. We take out the ruined material, dry the framing and leave the roof repair itself to a roofer.

Bathrooms: Bathroom floods usually come from a forgotten bathtub, a stopped-up sink or a toilet that will not quit rising. Tile looks waterproof, yet water gets through grout lines and around the edges of the room. In an upstairs bathroom the next stop is the ceiling of the room underneath. Anything that came out of the toilet bowl should be treated as dirty water and cleaned with disinfectant.

Walls: Plumbing hidden in a wall may seep at a joint for weeks without leaving a puddle anywhere. The hints are small: a baseboard that swells, a stain that grows, or a patch of wall that feels cool and soft. On these calls in Logan, an infrared camera and a pin meter show us how far the dampness reaches. Only that section is opened and dried, once a plumber has stopped the leak.

Tanks: A water heater holds a large volume under pressure, usually in a closet, garage or utility room. Drips from the relief valve, the drain valve or the fittings on top are early signs that parts are wearing. If the tank lets go, shut the cold inlet valve over the heater and turn off its power or gas supply. We pump out the spill and test the surrounding floor and wall bases, since water tends to run under them.

Drains: A clogged sewer line makes itself known at whichever drain sits lowest. If murky water comes up in a downstairs shower or floor drain, waste has nowhere to go but back inside. That water is Category 3, the most contaminated kind, and it takes protective equipment to clean up safely. Stop using water in the house and keep people and pets away until a trained crew arrives in Logan.

The First Hour After Water Damage in Logan

The first hour is when good intentions cause the most trouble. These are the mistakes our crews see most often in Logan, and what to do instead.

Never run an ordinary vacuum cleaner over a wet floor. It is an electric shock risk and it will ruin the machine; use towels and a mop. Leave ceiling fixtures off if the ceiling above is wet, and keep away from any part that droops. Leave fitted carpet in place, because pulling it up the wrong way can stretch or rip a carpet we might have saved. Avoid cranking up the heating, as warmth plus moisture encourages mold.

The helpful things are simple: close the valve, prop open cupboards and wardrobes, and stand wet cushions and textiles up to drain. Photograph each room before you shift a single item. Do keep children and pets away from the area. Most of all, phone early, so a Draper Restoration crew is already heading for Logan while you work through this list.

Simple Ways to Keep a Logan Home Dry

A flood in the house feels like it came from nowhere, yet most trace back to wear that built up over months or years. For homeowners in Logan, prevention is mainly a matter of knowing where to look.

Plumbing comes first. A water bill that climbs for no reason, or pressure that seems too high, can signal a concealed leak or strain on the system. Next come appliances. Do not run the washing machine or dishwasher when nobody is home, and replace the hoses on a schedule instead of waiting for one to fail. The roof and the ground outside are third. Missing shingles, cracked flashing, blocked gutters and soil that slopes toward the house all let rain find a way in.

Going away for more than a few days? Turn the water off at the main, or ask someone to check the house. Homeowners in Logan can ask Draper Restoration about any of this. If something does fail, calling quickly is what keeps the damage contained.
